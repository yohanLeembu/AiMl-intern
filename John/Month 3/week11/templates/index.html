Fashion sketch classifier

Draw a clothing item and the Week 10 neural network will guess what it is. It knows ten kinds: T-shirt/top, trouser, pullover, dress, coat, sandal, shirt, sneaker, bag and ankle boot.

Your prediction will show up here.

  • Draw a solid, filled-in shape, not just an outline. The training photos are filled silhouettes.
  • Make it big. The drawing is cropped and resized automatically, so position doesn't matter.
  • A sketch looks different from a photo, so expect some wrong guesses.
